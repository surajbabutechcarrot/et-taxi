Aligned with the UAE’s sustainability goals, the fleet includes more than 250 modern hybrid vehicles, demonstrating Emirates Transport’s commitment to eco-friendly mobility solutions. The fleet also features seven-seater taxis and vehicles specially designed for People of Determination. All vehicles are equipped with advanced meters, in-car surveillance cameras, and tracking systems, and accept both cash and card payments, fully complying with the regulations of the Ajman Transport Authority.
